BioFIA operates at the intersection of five interconnected systems — technology, ecological science, market design, governance, and policy. Our working groups co-design solutions across all five, because no single intervention alone can close the finance gap for standing forests.

Five Systems · One Alliance

Where we intervene

Protecting primary tropical forests is not a single-domain problem. It requires simultaneous breakthroughs in technology, science, finance, law, and policy. BioFIA convenes working groups across all five systems — each one advancing a distinct challenge, each one connected to the others.

Click any working group below to explore what we're building.

Working Group 01
Platform & Infrastructure Technology
Designing the technical architecture that makes forest protection legible to global capital markets — from blockchain protocols and smart contracts to wallets, data oracles, and low-bandwidth apps for remote communities.

Working Group 02
Ecological Science & TEK / Forest Protectors

Our Approach

A new kind of
conservation alliance

BioFIA is not a project developer, a fund, or a standard-setter. We are the governance and convening layer — the infrastructure that makes all of it possible and trustworthy.

🌿
Communities as Protagonists
Forest protectors are not beneficiaries — they are the architects and decision-makers of this system. FPIC is continuous, not a checkbox. Data sovereignty is non-negotiable.
⛓
Protocol-Level Trust
Royalty flows, verification standards, and governance rules are enforced at the smart contract layer — not by intermediaries. Bitcoin-level trust for forest finance.
🔬
Science-Backed Standards
Every SFT and AVT is backed by rigorous ecological verification. BioFIA sets accreditation standards for verifiers — and enforces them through community legitimacy, not legal contracts alone.
📐
Open Protocol, Open Market
The SFT protocol is open. Any accredited verifier can compete in our marketplace. No single entity controls the flow of capital — not even BioFIA.
Flagship Project

The Standing Forest Token ecosystem

The SFT is BioFIA's first major intervention — a dual-token blockchain protocol that converts verified primary forest protection into a tradeable digital asset, generating permanent royalties for forest protectors on every trade, forever.

This is not another carbon credit. It is financial infrastructure for a new asset class: standing primary tropical forest as a global reserve of natural capital.

Learn more at standingforests.org
Dual-Token Architecture
SFT · Fungible
Standing Forest Token
1 token = 1 hectare of verified primary tropical forest. Tradeable. Finite. Generates perpetual royalties for the forest protector on every trade.
AVT · Non-Fungible
Annual Verification Token
Minted annually per hectare. A customizable bundle of ecosystem services — carbon, biodiversity, water, soil, community — defined by the forest protector.
SFT traded on exchange → royalty captured → distributed to forest protectors proportionally
Forest verified annually → AVT minted to protector → sold to supporters → retired for certified contribution
Supporters stake SFTs → gain brand visibility on forest profile → obligation to purchase annual AVTs
