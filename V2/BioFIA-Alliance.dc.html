Ancestral futures

The communities who have protected these forests for generations are not stakeholders to consult. They are the intelligence layer of the system, carrying ancestral ways of knowing that have sustained these ecosystems across centuries and that guide where we go next.

Indigenous wisdom is not a legacy to preserve. It is a living design principle for the world we are building.
